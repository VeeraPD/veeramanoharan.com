# People, voice and music provenance

[Maya](maya.png), [Daniel](daniel.png) and [Elena](elena.png) are fictional AI-generated portraits, not real employees or customer endorsements. [Exact portrait prompts](portrait-prompts.json) record the original generation mode and descriptions. Elena's original portrait prompt said service leader; current narration and labels use IT service manager.

The narrator is Microsoft Aria synthetic speech reading an original script. The voice does not imitate the portfolio owner. The music is an original procedural instrumental with no third-party recording, samples or external melody used; [music provenance](music-provenance.json) records its source WAV hash and parameters. The audio is baked into the final films. The source WAV is not duplicated in this pack; [the generator snapshot](../../references/code/music-generator.py) documents how it was created in the repository.
